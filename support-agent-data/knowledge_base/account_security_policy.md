# Account & Security Policy

## Password & Login
- Reset your password from Account Settings > Security > Reset Password — a reset link is emailed and expires after 30 minutes.
- After 5 failed login attempts, the account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes as a security measure.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Enable 2FA from Account Settings > Security > 2FA (SMS or authenticator app supported).
- If you lose access to your 2FA device, identity verification (email + order history confirmation) is required to regain access.

## Account Lockouts & Suspicious Activity
- Logins from a new device or location may trigger a verification email for your protection.
- If your account is locked due to suspicious activity, we email you immediately with next steps.
- Identity verification for a locked account typically takes under 24 hours once you respond with the requested details.

## Updating Account Information
- Email address: Account Settings > Profile > Update Email (requires verifying the new email).
- Password: Account Settings > Security > Change Password.
- Phone number: Account Settings > Profile > Update Phone (used for delivery updates and 2FA SMS).

## Data Privacy
- We do not sell or share personal data with third parties without consent, other than service providers needed to fulfill orders (payment processors, shipping carriers).
- You can request a copy of your stored personal data by emailing privacy@company.com.
- Account and data deletion requests are processed within 48 hours of a verified request.
- Order history required for tax/legal purposes may be retained in anonymized form even after account deletion.

## Session & Device Management
- View and log out of active sessions from Account Settings > Security > Active Sessions.
- Logging out of all devices is recommended if you suspect unauthorized access, followed by a password reset.

# Billing & Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Rupay).
- PayPal.
- UPI (India only).
- Buy Now, Pay Later via partner providers (available at checkout for eligible orders).

## Charges & Holds
- A temporary authorization hold may appear at checkout before the final charge posts — this is normal and clears within 1-3 business days.
- Duplicate-looking charges are usually one authorization hold + one final charge, not an actual double charge. Confirm with your bank statement before disputing.
- If genuinely charged twice for the same order beyond 3 business days, contact support with both transaction IDs for investigation and refund.

## Subscriptions
- Subscriptions renew automatically each billing cycle (monthly or annual, as selected).
- Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge: Account > Billing > Manage Subscription > Cancel.
- Cancelling mid-cycle does not provide a prorated refund for the current period; access continues until the period ends.
- Failed subscription renewal payments trigger 3 retry attempts over 7 days before the subscription is paused.

## Invoices & Receipts
- Invoices are available immediately after purchase from Account > Billing History > Download Invoice.
- Business/GST invoices with company details can be requested by adding billing info in Account > Billing > Business Details before purchase.

## Discounts & Promotions
- Student discount: 10% off with a valid student email verification (one-time verification, reusable).
- Bulk order discount: 15% off for orders of 20+ units of the same item, applied automatically at checkout.
- Promo codes are single-use per account unless stated otherwise, and cannot be combined with other active promotions.
- Price-match requests must be submitted within 7 days of purchase with proof of the lower price from an authorized retailer.

## Payment Failures
- If a payment fails but a hold still appears on your statement, it will reverse automatically within 2-3 business days — no action needed unless it persists.
- Repeated payment failures may be due to bank fraud filters on international transactions; contact your bank or try an alternate payment method.

## Currency & Taxes
- Prices are displayed in your local currency where supported; otherwise USD with card-network conversion applied by your bank.
- Sales tax/VAT is calculated at checkout based on shipping destination.

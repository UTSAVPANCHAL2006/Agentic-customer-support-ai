# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Methods & Timelines
- **Standard shipping:** 5-7 business days, $4.99 (free on orders over $50).
- **Express shipping:** 2-3 business days, $12.99.
- **Same-day delivery:** available in select metro areas for orders placed before 11 AM, $19.99.
- **International shipping:** 10-15 business days, rates vary by destination country, available to 40+ countries.

## Order Cutoff & Processing
- Orders placed before 2 PM (local warehouse time) ship the same business day.
- Orders placed after 2 PM, or on weekends/holidays, ship the next business day.
- Custom or made-to-order items may add 2-5 extra processing days before shipping.

## Delivery Partners
- Domestic: FedEx, UPS, and regional couriers for last-mile delivery.
- International: DHL Express and local postal services depending on destination.

## Order Tracking
- A tracking link is emailed once the order ships.
- Tracking can also be viewed from Account > Order History > Track Order.
- Tracking updates may take 24-48 hours to first appear after shipment.

## Delivery Issues
- **Marked delivered but not received:** wait 24 hours (sometimes carriers scan early), then contact support with the order ID — we'll file a carrier claim.
- **Package damaged in transit:** report within 48 hours of delivery with photos for a free replacement or refund.
- **Delayed shipment:** if a package exceeds the estimated delivery window by more than 3 days, contact support for an update or compensation (e.g., free express upgrade on next order).

## Address Changes
- Shipping address can be changed only before the order status moves to "processing" or "shipped."
- Once shipped, address changes must go through the carrier directly (we can provide the tracking/carrier contact).

## Customs & International Duties
- International orders may be subject to customs duties/import taxes, which are the customer's responsibility unless the order was placed under a Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) promotion.
- Customs delays are outside our control and not covered under delivery-time guarantees.

## Shipping Restrictions
- Some items (batteries, aerosols, certain electronics) cannot be shipped internationally due to carrier restrictions.
- P.O. Box delivery is supported for standard shipping only, not express or same-day.

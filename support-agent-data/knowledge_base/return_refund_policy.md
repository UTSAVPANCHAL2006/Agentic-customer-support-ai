# Return & Refund Policy

## Return Window
- Standard return window: 30 days from the delivery date.
- Electronics (laptops, smartwatches, headphones): 15-day return window due to depreciation risk.
- Furniture and large items (chairs, desks): 45-day return window due to longer inspection time.
- Sale/clearance items: final sale, no returns unless defective.

## Conditions for a Valid Return
- Item must be unused, unworn, and in original packaging with tags attached.
- All original accessories, manuals, and freebies must be included.
- Proof of purchase (order ID or receipt) is required.
- Items showing signs of use, damage not caused by shipping, or missing parts may be rejected or refunded partially.

## Non-Returnable Categories
- Perishable goods (food, plants).
- Digital goods (software licenses, gift cards, downloadable content).
- Personalized or custom-made items (engraved, monogrammed, made-to-order).
- Intimate/hygiene products (underwear, earbuds without seal, cosmetics once opened).

## Refund Process
- Refund is initiated once the returned item passes warehouse inspection (1-2 business days after receipt).
- Refund processing time: 5-7 business days after inspection approval.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method. UPI/PayPal refunds may take an additional 2-3 days to reflect.
- Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective item, damaged in transit).

## Exchanges
- Exchanges are allowed within the applicable return window, subject to stock availability.
- If the requested exchange item is out of stock, a refund is issued instead.
- Size/color exchanges for clothing and footwear are free of additional shipping charges (one exchange per order).

## Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Item Received
- Report within 7 days of delivery with photos for fastest resolution.
- We cover return shipping costs in these cases.
- Priority processing: replacement or refund is issued within 2 business days of approval, without waiting for the item to be returned first (for confirmed defects/wrong items).

## Late or Missing Refunds
- If a refund hasn't appeared after 10 business days, check with your bank/card issuer first — processing delays sometimes occur on their end.
- If still missing after 10 business days, contact support with the order ID and payment method for investigation.

## Exceptions
- Requests outside the standard window are evaluated case by case by support, considering factors like loyalty tier, reason for delay, and item condition.
